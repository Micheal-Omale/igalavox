Enwukedogba

/E-nwu-ke-do-gba/

Meaning

Heart desire

Emerging from the depths of cultural heritage, this name signifies the profound manifestation of one's inner aspirations and the fulfillment of ancestral promises. It is often bestowed upon children who represent a long-awaited blessing within the lineage.

Related Proverb

"The heart that knows its desire finds the path even through the densest forest."
Region

West Africa

Era
